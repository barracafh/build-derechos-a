# Backlog: third-sector resources for children and families

**Status: wanted, not built.** Nothing here is in the app, the central store or the seeds. It is a list the team gathered on 22 Oct 2026 of programmes run by NGOs and foundations (school support, childhood, family support) that a citizen in València could be sent to, as a possible new source after the Build night.

Why it is separate from S4: these are not public aids with requisites and deadlines but services of private entities. They need their own shape (entity, programme, category, services, area, how to access, cost), their own verification rule (what the entity's page confirms versus what is pending) and a decision on whether the chat may recommend non-public resources at all.

Contact data (phones, emails) is deliberately left out of this file: it lives on each source URL, and the event's personal-data check flags any phone or email in versioned files.

## Before building anything

- Decide whether the chat may suggest private resources, and how it labels them (never "podrías optar", which is for public aids).
- Agree the verification rule: a resource is shown only with a source page that confirms the programme; cost and places stay "ask the entity" unless the page states them.
- Pick the shape: probably a new `Ficha` source with `fuente: "recurso"` and its own card, after public aids and BDNS calls.
- Cover: 6 of the 10 are in València; YMCA +Infancia has no site in the Comunitat Valenciana today.
